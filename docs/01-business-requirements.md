# ARTIBOTS × PREETHI’S DENTISTRY
## Document 01: Business Requirements & Commercial Objectives

**Technology Partner:** Artibots Innovation Private Limited  
**Flagship Client:** Dr. Preethi’s Dentistry, Saibaba Colony, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India  
**Target Market:** Urban Tier-1/Tier-2 Dental & Orthodontic Patients in South India  

---

### 1. Commercial Objectives

1. **High-Intent Patient Acquisition**:
   - Drive patient inquiries specifically for high-value aesthetic and orthodontic treatments (**Clear Aligners & Invisalign®**, Cosmetic Veneers, Single-Visit Restorations).
   - Educate prospective patients on modern **needle-free anesthesia** to eliminate dental anxiety and boost conversion rates.
2. **Operational Efficiency & Chair Utilization**:
   - Reduce clinic front-desk administrative drag by automating routine consultation booking and rescheduling.
   - Enforce an operational **15-minute lead response SLA** to minimize lead drop-off.
3. **Patient Retention & Adherence**:
   - Track clear aligner tray progression systematically (Trays 1..N) to ensure wear compliance (20–22 hours daily).
   - Provide automated appointment confirmation slips and directions via WhatsApp.
4. **Artibots Commercial Scalability**:
   - Validate the core operational platform at Dr. Preethi's Dentistry before offering the Artibots Dental CRM as a subscription SaaS product to other clinics across India.

---

### 2. Patient Demographics & Key Stakeholders

- **Clear Aligner Demographic**: Working professionals, university students, and brides/grooms seeking discreet aesthetic alignment without metal brackets.
- **Pediatric & Anxious Patients**: Individuals seeking needle-free, painless dental anesthesia.
- **Clinical Director (Dr. Preethi Subramanian)**: Requires oversight over clinical outcomes, appointment density, and staff performance without being bogged down by manual record keeping.
- **Reception & Front Desk**: Requires an intuitive, fast queue to call inquiries, book slots, and dispatch reminders.

---

### 3. Business Rules & SLA Standards

1. **Operating Hours**:
   - Morning Session: `10:00 AM – 01:00 PM`
   - Evening Session: `05:00 PM – 08:00 PM`
   - Closed: Sundays / Declared Clinic Holidays
2. **Consultation Slot Duration**: Standardized at **30-minute** blocks.
3. **Double Booking Policy**: Strict zero-tolerance for double-booked chairs or dentists.
4. **Lead Response Protocol**:
   - Any lead remaining in `NEW` status for over 15 minutes during operating hours triggers an alert in the operational dashboard.
5. **Medical Communication Lawfulness**: All patient outreach must be strictly transactional (appointment confirmations, rescheduling, care reminders) with explicit patient consent. No unsolicited commercial promotions without affirmative opt-in.
